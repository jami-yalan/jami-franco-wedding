Founding Exhibition

Yalán-Salazar Family

Permanent Collection · Nine Works

This exhibition brings together nine works from the permanent collection of Jamilet Yalán and Franco Salazar. The objects gathered here were not produced with museographic intent: they are everyday artefacts, photographic records, self-produced works, and documents of a story that began in a faculty of art in 2011 and that tonight reaches one of its most publicly visible moments. The collection is, by nature, permanent and perpetually unfinished.

IMagic: The Gathering Decks
MTG Deck · Vorel
[1]

Commander Deck: Vorel of the Hull Clade

Jamilet Yalánc. 2012 – present 1 commander · 4 selected cards Magic: The Gathering · Commander Yalán-Salazar Collection

Originally built from a Simic guild deck from "Return to Ravnica" during its owner's university years at the Faculty of Art, this deck has been continuously refined over more than a decade of active play. The choice of Vorel of the Hull Clade — a proliferation commander — reflects a sustained preference for adaptive growth that, in retrospect, might be read as biographical.

MTG Deck · Kykar
[2]

Commander Deck: Kykar, Wind's Fury

Franco Salazarc. 2012 – present 1 commander · 4 selected cards Magic: The Gathering · Commander Yalán-Salazar Collection

Originating from an old bird deck in 2012, later transformed into an Azorius deck, and subsequently restructured around Kykar, Wind's Fury — a Jeskai commander whose blue-red-white alignment represents a deliberate expansion beyond its origins. The deck is a living document of its builder's shifting aesthetic within the game.

IIFamily Portraits
Portrait · Jamilet
[3]

Portrait of Jamilet Yalán (unfinished)

Franco Salazar14 February 2025 Acrylic on canvas Yalán-Salazar Collection

Painted on Valentine's Day 2025 as part of a simultaneous portrait exchange. Salazar's treatment — simplified colour planes and an economy of line that achieves a recognisable likeness — places the work within an expressionist tradition of intimate portraiture. The piece was incorporated in an unfinished state, a condition the artist has not ruled out resolving at some future date.

Portrait · Franco
[4]

Portrait of Franco Salazar

Jamilet Yalán14 February 2025 Acrylic on canvas Yalán-Salazar Collection

Produced in the same session as [3], this work takes a markedly different approach: bluish greys, greater attention to volume and shadow, and a treatment closer to classical portraiture. The divergence between the two pieces — executed simultaneously in the same medium — constitutes an involuntary document of each artist's pictorial sensibility.

El Pupi · Technical Pen
[5]

Captain Prick Von Popplewell, "El Pupi"

Franco SalazarDate unknown Technical pen on paper Yalán-Salazar Collection

An informal piece incorporated as an unannounced addition. The subject — a domestic feline and permanent member of the household — appears with Señor Platanito, his personal play object. Its inclusion responds to criteria distinct from the rest of the collection, without diminishing its documentary value.

Archive of Recent Years (2023–2026)

Yalán-Salazar · Joint production 2023–2026 3 Instax Mini · 1 analogue strip · 1 glass jar Yalán-Salazar Collection

Four years of shared life through a selection of photographs and objects: the last image before departure to the United Kingdom (2023); their tenth anniversary in Scotland (2024); an analogue photo-booth strip from Manchester (2025); and the last print before the wedding (2026). The jar contains hair from El Pupi — present by material proxy since he cannot attend the ceremony.

Projected Archive: Three Collections

Jamilet Yalán · Compilation & editing Add. photography: Fernando Roncal (Lima) c. 1960s–2026 · Looped video · With audio Projected on fabric · Approx. 5 min Yalán-Salazar Collection

Three collections in a single continuous projection: pre-wedding portraits by Fernando Roncal; photographs of the co-founder's grandmothers and mother on their wedding days; and an archive of founding moments — first apartment, second apartment, engagement. The loop has no beginning or end: three generations of women and an ongoing story coexist simultaneously.
