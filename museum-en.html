Yalán-Salazar
Family

Permanent Collection · Nine Works

Nine works from the permanent collection of Jamilet Yalán and Franco Salazar. The objects gathered here were not produced with museographic intent: everyday artefacts, photographic records, self-produced works, and documents of a story that began in a faculty of art in 2011 and reaches tonight one of its most publicly visible moments.

Vorel · Commander
[1]

Commander Deck: Vorel of the Hull Clade

Jamilet Yalán c. 2012 – present 1 commander · 4 selected cards Yalán-Salazar Collection

Originally built from a Simic guild deck from "Return to Ravnica," this deck has been refined over more than a decade. The choice of Vorel of the Hull Clade — a proliferation commander — reflects a sustained preference for adaptive growth that, in retrospect, might be read as biographical.

Kykar · Commander
[2]

Commander Deck: Kykar, Wind's Fury

Franco Salazar c. 2012 – present 1 commander · 4 selected cards Yalán-Salazar Collection

Originating from an old bird deck in 2012, transformed into an Azorius deck, and subsequently restructured around Kykar, Wind's Fury — a Jeskai commander whose blue-red-white alignment represents a deliberate expansion beyond its origins. A living document of its builder's shifting aesthetic.

Portrait · Jamilet
[3]

Portrait of Jamilet Yalán (unfinished)

Franco Salazar 14 February 2025 Acrylic on canvas Yalán-Salazar Collection

Painted as part of a simultaneous portrait exchange. Salazar's treatment — simplified colour planes and an economy of line achieving a recognisable likeness — places the work within an expressionist tradition of intimate portraiture. Incorporated in an unfinished state.

Portrait · Franco
[4]

Portrait of Franco Salazar

Jamilet Yalán 14 February 2025 Acrylic on canvas Yalán-Salazar Collection

Produced in the same session as [3], with a markedly different approach: bluish greys, greater volume and shadow, and a treatment closer to classical portraiture. The divergence between the two pieces constitutes an involuntary document of each artist's pictorial sensibility.

El Pupi · Technical Pen
[5]

Captain Prick Von Popplewell, known as "The Pupi"

Franco Salazar Date unknown Technical pen on paper Yalán-Salazar Collection

The subject — a domestic feline and permanent member of the household — appears with Mr. Platanito, his personal play object. Its inclusion responds to criteria distinct from the rest of the collection, without diminishing its documentary value.

Archive · 2023–2026
[6]

Archive of Recent Years

Yalán-Salazar · 2023–2026 3 Instax Mini · 1 analogue strip · 1 glass jar Yalán-Salazar Collection

Four years of shared life through a selection of photographs and objects: the last image before departure to the United Kingdom (2023); their tenth anniversary in Scotland (2024); an analogue photo-booth strip from Manchester (2025); and the last photographic print before the wedding (2026). The jar contains hair from the Pupi — present by material proxy since he cannot attend the ceremony in person.

IVFrom Families of Origin to Our Own

From Families of Origin to Our Own Family: Photographic Triptych

Various authors · 2000–2022 3 framed printed photographs Yalán-Salazar Collection

Three photographs charting a biographical arc: two childhood portraits from the collectors' families of origin, and a joint portrait documenting the family they built together. Arranged with the childhood portraits above and the joint portrait below, the triptych proposes convergence rather than descent.

VProjected Archive: Three Collections
Projected Video · Loop · c. 5 min
[10]

Projected Archive: Three Collections

Jamilet Yalán · Compilation & editing Additional photography: Fernando Roncal (Lima) c. 1960s–2026 · Looped video · With audio Projected on fabric · Approx. 5 min Yalán-Salazar Collection

Three collections in a single continuous projection: pre-wedding portraits by Fernando Roncal; photographs of the co-founder's grandmothers and mother on their wedding days; and an archive of founding moments — first apartment, second apartment, engagement. The loop has no beginning or end: three generations of women and an ongoing story coexist simultaneously.
