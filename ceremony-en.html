Welcome

Good evening everyone, and the very warmest welcome to this wonderful moment in Jami and Franco's life together. They are getting married! — and we are all here to support them and witness the vows they will make to each other. Our presence and our love is our gift to them.

A very special welcome to those of you who came all the way from Peru and Canada to Scotland to be with Franco and Jami in this important moment.

My name is Gerrie Douglas-Scott and I am a Celebrate People celebrant, and it is my privilege to conduct their ceremony on this special day. Celebrate People is exactly that — to celebrate these two very loved people in your life, in the way that means most to them.

Jami and Franco told me they chose a Celebrate People humanist ceremony because they feel it best expresses their feelings towards each other. They wanted a personal ceremony that reflects who they are, and allows them to say in their own words why they love one another, what their hopes are for the future, and what they promise to one another.

They told me they wanted a spiritual ceremony, filled with the energy they themselves create — without losing sight of all that this moment means for both of them.

The Candles

To begin their ceremony, Jami and Franco will each light a candle — representing their own unique and individual selves that they are bringing to their marriage. When they have made their vows to each other, they will take the flames from their candles and together light a unity candle, as a symbol of their oneness and their marriage.

On Love & Marriage

I asked them to tell me what love and marriage mean to them. Every couple I speak with gives me a different answer — and you, no doubt, have your own answer too.

They found their answer in verses by Roberto Juarroz, and they wanted to share them with you.

Roberto Juarroz

We have loved so many things together that it is hard to love them apart. They seem to have suddenly moved away, or as if love were an ant scaling the slopes of the sky. We have lived together through so much abyss that without you everything seems surface, an orbit of simulacra that slips away, tension without extension, a vigil of bodies without presence. We have walked so far without moving that journeys now hang loose like useless coats. Movement and stillness have come undone like degrees of two temperatures. We have lost so much nothingness together that the habit persists and turns around and now everything is gain from nothingness. Time becomes anti-time because you no longer think it. We have been silent and spoken so much together that even silence and speech are two betrayals, two substances without justification, two substitutes. We have sought everything, we have found everything, we have let go of everything.

Their Story

Now, let us hear a little of their story — the journey that brought them here, to this golden hour.

Franco and Jami's love story is one of patience, friendship, and finding home in each other. They met at university and spent years as close friends before life brought them together romantically. After both had ended previous relationships, they naturally gravitated towards one another. What began as two friends being there for each other blossomed into something deeper — rooted in belonging. Their journey took them from Lima to Edinburgh in 2023, where they built a life together from scratch, just the two of them. Without family nearby, they became each other's anchor: Jami providing Franco with unwavering support and direction, whilst Franco shows his devotion through constant care and thoughtfulness.

Franco proposed to Jami at sunset by the sea, the day after her birthday, in a hotel room with a private pool overlooking the ocean. That golden hour moment became their symbol — the same light they used to watch together from their first flat in Miraflores, and the same magical glow of Lima's legendary sunsets that they want to recreate on their wedding day.

Their story isn't about grand gestures or love at first sight — it's about two best friends who chose each other, who built a home thousands of miles from where they started, and who found that together, the unfamiliar never feels frightening. Because they are each other's family and home, and the reason no place ever feels too far or too unknown.
